Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information HomeVault may collect, how it is used, how it is protected, and the choices available to you.

01 Information We Collect

HomeVault may collect information that you provide when creating an account, requesting a service, communicating with us, or using our inventory tools.

Account Information

Name, email address, contact details, and account-related information.

Property Information

Details you choose to record about your home and belongings.

Photos & Records

Images, descriptions, receipts, and documents you upload.

Usage Information

Information about how you interact with supported features.

02 How Information Is Collected

Information may be collected directly from you when you use HomeVault features or communicate with our team.

  • When you create or update an account.
  • When you upload inventory photos or supporting documentation.
  • When you schedule or request a service.
  • When you contact HomeVault support.
  • Through technical information generated when interacting with the website.

03 How We Use Information

Information may be used to provide, maintain, improve, and personalize HomeVault services.

  • Create and manage your digital inventory.
  • Generate inventory documentation and reports.
  • Process service requests and scheduling.
  • Communicate with you about your account or requested services.
  • Maintain website security and reliability.

04 Photos & Personal Documents

HomeVault may allow you to upload photographs, receipts, product information, and other documentation related to household belongings.

You should only upload information that you are comfortable storing through the service and should avoid uploading unnecessary sensitive information.

Important: Keep independent copies of important photographs, receipts, insurance records, and other documents that you cannot afford to lose.

05 Information Sharing

HomeVault does not treat your inventory information as public content simply because it is stored within the platform.

Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to deliver a service you request, or where required to comply with applicable legal obligations.

Third-party providers may have their own privacy policies and terms that apply to their services.

06 Data Security

HomeVault uses reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

No internet-based service can guarantee absolute security. Users should also take reasonable precautions to protect their account credentials and devices.

07 Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide requested services, maintain appropriate records, resolve disputes, comply with legal requirements, or fulfill other legitimate operational purposes.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the purpose for which it was collected.

08 Your Privacy Choices

Depending on the features available to you, you may have options to review, update, or request changes to certain account information.

  • Update inaccurate account information.
  • Request information about your stored personal data.
  • Request account closure where applicable.
  • Manage certain communication preferences.

Questions about your privacy?

Contact the HomeVault support team for privacy-related questions or requests.
